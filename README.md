# Offshore-tooling-for-injection-molding
Offshore tooling for injection molding

Offshore tooling for injection molding, through a vetted partner factory network
[Offshore tooling](https://jchtrade.com/partner-factory-network) for injection molding means the mold is built, and usually run, at a partner tool shop abroad rather than in a US toolroom. JCH matches each tool to a vetted partner factory whose everyday work fits the part, and Jack provides the engineering and QC oversight: the tool is specified and reviewed before steel is cut, then checked at first article, in process and before shipment.

Matched to the part, not the price list
Jack works through a network of vetted partner factories. Each one is visited, its recent work is reviewed, and its strengths are recorded by process: mold building, injection molding, insert molding and overmolding, sheet metal, sewn goods, packaging and electronics assembly.

When your program arrives, the shortlist comes from that record. You hear which partner factory, why that one, and what would change the answer, before any steel is cut.
